2. Verify candidate IDs and analytic geometry in the registry. Compare surface/curve type, direction, radius when applicable, scale, and adjacency.
3. For axis-based joints, prefer a cylindrical face or circular/arc edge that directly represents the intended axis over a nearby planar end cap. For planar motion, prefer the functional planar face rather than an incidental small cap.
